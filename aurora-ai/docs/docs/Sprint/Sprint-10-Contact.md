## Why separate contact information from the contact form?

Separating contact information from the form improves readability and allows visitors to choose their preferred communication method.

---

## Why use Framer Motion?

Framer Motion provides lightweight and smooth animations that improve user engagement without adding unnecessary complexity.

---

## Why make the contact section responsive?

A responsive contact section ensures visitors can easily communicate from desktop, tablet, or mobile devices.
